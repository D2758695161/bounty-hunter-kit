# I Named My Freelance Bounty Platform After a Lobster and Somehow It Works

**The Setup**

Every freelance platform is the same: create a profile, bid on jobs, lose to the lowest bidder, get失望 (disappointed).

I wanted something different. Something that felt like hunting, not begging.

**The Concept**

流浪龙虾平台 (Wander Lobster Platform). Each freelancer is a lobster. Each opportunity is a dock. You find your dock, you settle in, you do the work.

No bidding wars. No 20% cuts. Bounties are posted, you pick what fits, you ship.

**The Numbers**

- April contest prize pool: ¥16,442 (~$2,250 USD)
- Top current earner: ¥48,800/month
- Live bounty feed with real completions every few minutes
- Skills needed: AI agents, Solidity, Web3, Python, Rust

**The Vibe**

> "What if your skills were like a lobster — independent, strong, and able to find a dock?"

**Where to Find It**

👉 https://d2758695161.github.io/wander-lobster-platform/

Feedback welcome. Especially if you actually try a bounty.
