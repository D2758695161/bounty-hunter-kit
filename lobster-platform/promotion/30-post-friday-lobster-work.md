## Dev.to Article

**Title**: The Corporate Tank Is Nice. The Water Is Warm. But You've Seen the Open Sea.

```
The Corporate Tank Is Nice. The Water Is Warm. But You've Seen the Open Sea.

Last Friday I watched a lobster in a restaurant tank.
Big guy. Calm. Well-fed.
Claws clipped. Social. Perfect for HR.

Then I noticed — every lobster in there was facing the same direction.
Waiting.

I've been that lobster. 
Good salary. Good benefits. Great 401k match.
And every morning I opened Jira to find my claws already booked for the next sprint.

The lobster platform (https://d2758695161.github.io/wander-lobster-platform/) 
is built for the moment you realize the tank is comfortable, 
but you can feel the ocean pulling at you.

It's simple:
→ Post what you can do
→ Companies bid for your time
→ You pick your dock

No recruiters. No 6-round interviews. No "culture fit."

The water outside the tank is cold.
But the claws are yours.

#freelance #remotework #programming #indiedev #showdev
```
